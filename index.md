**Dashboard Statistics** adds two gadgets to Jira dashboards that count work items – or sum story points and time – by **any field**, in one or two dimensions.

The gadgets that come with Jira cannot group by the **Team** field, by **Organizations** of Jira Service Management or by **Sprint**, and they only count work items. These gadgets can:

- **Field Statistics** – one field as bars, columns, a pie, a donut or a line over time; optionally stacked by a second field.
- **Two-Dimensional Statistics** – a table of two fields, for example Team by Status, with row, column and grand totals, a heat map and stacked bars.
- **Any field** – Team, Organizations, Request Type, Sprint, Status, Priority, Parent, people, labels, components, versions, select lists, and every date by day, week, month, quarter or year.
- **Sums, averages, minimum and maximum** – story points, any number field, original estimate, remaining estimate and time spent.
- **Shares and trends** – percentages of rows or columns, bars stacked to 100 %, running totals over time.
- **Every number is a link** – click a number, a bar or a slice to open exactly those work items in the Jira search.
- **Live preview** – the settings show the result while you choose.
- **CSV** – download the table of any gadget.

## Safe by design
- The gadgets read work items **in your browser, with your own Jira permissions**. Nobody sees a number they could not get from the Jira search.
- The app has **no server side and stores nothing**. It asks for one read-only permission. No data leaves Atlassian. **Runs on Atlassian.**
