This policy describes how the Atlassian Marketplace app "Dashboard Statistics – Team, Organization & Sum Gadgets for Jira" (the "App") processes data. The App is provided by **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, Company ID (IČO) 21540951, registered office 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("we"). Contact: **[support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net)**.

## 1. What the App stores
Nothing. The App has no storage and no server-side code. The settings of a gadget – the id and name of a saved filter or a JQL search, the chosen fields and display options – are stored by Jira itself as properties of the dashboard.

## 2. What the App reads
When a gadget is shown, the App reads, in the viewer's browser and **with the viewer's Jira permissions**, the list of fields, the chosen saved filter and the values of the chosen fields of the work items the filter finds. These values may include names of people (for example assignees) when such a field is chosen. They are counted in the browser and shown to that viewer; they are not sent or stored anywhere. The App cannot change anything in Jira.

## 3. Where the data is processed
In the viewer's browser and in Atlassian's infrastructure that answers the Jira REST API. The App does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and we (the vendor) have no access to it.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis
Data is processed solely to provide the App's functions on behalf of the Jira site, which acts as the controller; we act as processor (Art. 28 GDPR).

## 5. Retention
The App keeps no data. Gadget settings stay in Jira until the gadget or its dashboard is deleted.

## 6. Data Processing Addendum
The [Data Processing Addendum]({{ '/dpa/' | relative_url }}) (Art. 28 GDPR) applies to all customers.

## 7. Sharing
We do not sell or share data. Sub-processor: Atlassian (hosting of the Forge platform).

## 8. Your rights
For requests concerning personal data, contact your Jira site admin or us at [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).
